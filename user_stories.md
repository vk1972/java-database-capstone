# User Story Template

**Title:**
## Admin User Stories
As a Admin, I want Log into the portal with username and password to manage the platform securely 
As a Admin, I want Log out of the portal to protect system access 
As a Admin, I want to Add doctors to the portal 
As a Admin, I want to Delete doctor's profile from the portal 
As a Admin, I want to Run a stored procedure in MySQL CLI to get the number of appointments per month and track usage statistics


## Patient User Stories
    As a patient I want to View a list of doctors without logging in to explore options before registering
    As a patient I want to Sign up using your email and password to book appointments
    As a patient I want to Log into the portal to manage your bookings
    As a patient I want to Log out of the portal to secure your account
    As a patient I want to Log in and book an hour-long appointment to consult with a doctor
    As a patient I want to View my upcoming appointments so that I can prepare accordingly

## Doctor User Stories#
        As a doctor I want to Log into the portal to manage your appointments
    As a doctor I want to Log out of the portal to protect my data
    As a doctor I want to View my appointment calendar to stay organized
    As a doctor I want to Mark your unavailability to inform patients only the available slots
    As a doctor I want to Update your profile with specialization and contact information so that patients have up-to-date information
    As a doctor I want to View the patient details for upcoming appointments so that I can be prepared
